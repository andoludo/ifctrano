    Use different libraries

    ifctrano generates models for four Modelica libraries:

    LIBRARY Library Zone model
    Buildings (default) Buildings Detailed: every surface, layer and window
    IDEAS IDEAS Detailed: every surface, layer and window
    reduced_order AixLib Reduced order: resistance–capacitance network
    iso_13790 AixLib ISO 13790 5R1C simple hourly method

    The library is given to save_model:

    from pathlib import Path

from ifctrano.building import Building

building = Building.from_ifc(Path("TwoZones.ifc"))
building.save_model(library="IDEAS")

    or as the second argument of ifctrano create:

    ifctrano create TwoZones.ifc IDEAS

    AixLib models

    The reduced_order and iso_13790 models simulate with --simulate-model since trano 0.43.0, which loads AixLib and installs it in the OpenModelica container. CI simulates the two zones model in all four libraries (tests/test_simulation.py); this comparison shows the two detailed libraries.

    Days 3 to 10 of the year (the first 2 simulated days are a warm-up and are left out).

    Case Zone Mean [°C] Minimum [°C] Maximum [°C]
    Buildings Mean of the zones 8.9 6.0 13.9
    IDEAS Mean of the zones 8.8 5.8 13.8

    What to expect. Both libraries describe each wall, window and floor with the same constructions, so free-floating temperatures should be close; differences come from the models of the convection, the radiation exchange, the windows and the ground.

    What the simulation shows. Over days 3 to 10, the mean temperature of the zones is 8.9 °C with Buildings and 8.8 °C with IDEAS, with minima of 6.0 and 5.8 °C and maxima of 13.9 and 13.8 °C: the two detailed libraries agree within 0.2 K on this building.
